# Detector event rules chosen from two replayed nights (October 2026)

Status: analysis for Labor 0.9.5 (versionCode 47), branch `labor-detector-replay`, 2026-10-26.
Everything here was computed on a desktop JVM from recordings; nothing was run on a phone.

## In short

- **Recommended and implemented (Labor editions only): rule set V3** = start at the local floor +
  the excess setting (6.5 dB, unchanged) **and** a rise of at least **3.5 dB within 3 s**; the event
  ends when the level falls to within **3 dB of the floor frozen at its start**, or after **30 s**;
  after such a 30 s cut no new event starts until the level has dropped below the current
  floor + excess − 3 dB once (the *hold*).
- On the two nights: **no event longer than 30 s** (before: 42 and 11 events, up to 124 s);
  **all 35 labelled vehicle / loud-vehicle events still detected** (34 with a start within ±2 s,
  the 35th, a 112 s truck/bus event, starts 15 s later); events 22–06 −7 % and −8 %.
- **Not reached: «drop at least half of the < 10 dB swells».** V3 drops 11 % of them. No rule
  tested can drop half while keeping ≥ 95 % of the labelled vehicles, because by the batch-5
  labels the < 10 dB events *are* vehicles: 15 of the 16 labelled events below 10 dB are
  vehicle or loud vehicle. Raising the excess is the only lever found, and it costs vehicles
  (8 dB: 22 of 35 kept). Whether distant traffic should count is a definition question (decisions.md
  §6, constant *a*), not a detector rule.
- The public editions (Offline-Version, Online-Version) are **unchanged**: the detector code is
  shared (android/dsp), but its default stays the old rules (V2); only the Labor editions pass V3,
  with a Labor switch back to V2 for A/B nights.

## Terms

- **LAF**: the A-weighted sound level with the «Fast» (125 ms) time constant, the level the
  detector looks at every 125 ms («tick»).
- **Local floor**: the level exceeded 90 % of the time (L90) over the last 30 s of LAF — the hum
  under the events. The detector freezes it when an event starts.
- **Excess**: how far LAF must rise above the local floor to start an event (setting «Lärmspitze
  ab … über Grundrauschen», default 6.5 dB). Per event, «excess» also names LAFmax − floor.
- **Swell** (here): a detected event whose LAFmax is less than 10 dB above its floor.
- **Rise** (new): LAF now minus the lowest LAF of the previous 3 s.
- **Hold** (new): after an event was cut at 30 s, no new start until the level has dropped (see
  above), so one long swell is not counted as a series of events.
- **V2 / V3**: the rule sets `EventRules.V2` (detector v2 as in 0.4.0–0.9.4) and `EventRules.V3`.
- **A/B night**: a night recorded with the old rules for comparison with one with the new rules.

## Method

1. The AAC hour files were decoded to 16 kHz WAV (`ffmpeg -i X.m4a -ac 1 -ar 16000 X.wav`) and
   replayed through the production engine with `:dsp:replayNight` (each session's own
   calibration offset and event floor, `--head none`; the event head is logging only). New
   options: `--from/--to` (a part of a long session, on the session's own sample axis and tick
   grid), `--ticks` (writes every 125 ms tick: LAF, LAFmax, LAeq, valid) and `--rules` etc.
2. The rule variants were run on those ticks with the new `:dsp:replayRules`: the engine's own
   `LocalFloorEstimator` and `EventDetector`, in the engine's order, without repeating the audio
   path (seconds per variant instead of 3–7 min of engine per night).
3. **Check of the method:** with V2 the tick replay gives exactly the engine's events (250/250
   and 566/566, identical start and end samples and LAFmax), and the full engine with V3 gives
   exactly the tick replay's V3 events (235/235, 523/523). A unit test
   (`EventRulesTest.tickReplayReproducesTheEngine`) keeps it so.
4. A labelled event counts as **detected** if a replayed event starts within ±2 s of its start
   (the brief). Also shown: detected by **overlap** with the labelled event's span, because a
   rise rule or a higher excess can start the same pass more than 2 s later.
5. Counts are per clock hour of the event start (Europe/Zurich), 22:00–06:00. Wind events are
   included (as in the manifests); the wind split of V3 is given below.

Notes on the table:

- a12 has *more* events than a10 at 22:00 on Rossberg: with 12 dB the end level (floor + 9 dB) cuts
  two 22–24 s events into 3 and 5 fragments. Higher thresholds fragment long events.
- The end rule alone (end within 3 dB, cap 300 s, not in the table: max 185 s) makes events
  *longer*, because 3 dB is below V2's end (floor + 3.5 dB). The 30 s cap is what bounds them.
- Without the hold, a 15 s cap re-triggers inside long swells (+13 % events); with the hold
  +1 %. At 30 s the hold barely matters (479 vs 475), because the local floor has caught up with
  a swell by then; it is kept as cheap insurance for shorter caps or higher excess settings.

## 3. What the labels say

- **The < 10 dB events are mostly vehicles.** Of the 35 labelled vehicle/loud events, 15 are below
  10 dB (ros 560, 697, 718, 731, 802, 873, 918, 954, 1037, 1093; kal 103, 166, 223, 233, 313); of
  the 16 labelled events below 10 dB, 15 are vehicle/loud (the 16th, kal 247, is «nothing»). This
  follows from decisions.md §6 (a = 0 dB: any audible vehicle is «vehicle»).
- **The rise is not a vehicle feature.** Distant night pass-bys rise only 4–6 dB within 3 s
  (labelled Gieshübel vehicles: best rise within their first 10 s 4.2–16.3 dB, median 6.4), while
  the fastest risers are the «other» events: a click, phone handling, breathing and bed sheets
  (14–35 dB). A 6 dB rise therefore drops
  half of the vehicles (b6: 18/35) and keeps most «other». Only a weak rise (3.5 dB) is used, as a
  filter for the slowest creeping swells.
- **The older labels disagree** (compare_all.csv, before §6): there 7 Gieshübel events are called
  «distant traffic swell» and 8 are vehicles (car, truck, motorbike, «poser»). Excess 8 dB keeps
  2/7 of those swells and 8/8 of those vehicles; V3 keeps 6/7 and 8/8. If the older notion of a
  swell is what should be dropped, the excess (a setting today) is the lever — at the cost the
  batch-5 rows show.
- **Best reachable trade-off:** the largest swell reduction that keeps at least 34 of 35
  vehicle/loud events (by start) is the 3.5 dB rise: −11 % of the swells. Every tested rule that
  drops ≥ 50 % of them loses at least 13 of the 35 (a8: 22/35 kept, 37 % of the swells kept);
  dropping 42–45 % already loses 6–10 (a7.5: 29/35 and 55 %; b5: 25/35 and 58 %).

## 4. Recommendation: V3

`EventRules.V3` = rise ≥ 3.5 dB within 3 s, end < frozen floor + min(3 dB, excess − 3 dB), cap
30 s, hold; the excess and the event floor stay the existing settings.

| labelled vehicle + loud detected, start ±2 s | 35/35 | 34/35 (97 %) | ≥ 95 % ✔ |

The one start miss, ros 632 («truck or bus», loud, a 112 s V2 event from 23:18:57), is detected
by V3 15.4 s later, when the level rises fast enough; V3 reports 30 s of it. Whether the loud part
is inside those 30 s was not checked by listening.

Confirmed with the full engine (`:dsp:replayNight --rules v3`): 235 events Gieshübel and 523
Rossberg (21:00–07:00), identical to the tick replay.

The Labor clips (±5 s around the event, at most 60 s) are no longer cut by long events: V3
events are at most 30 s.

## 5. Implementation

- `android/dsp/.../EventDetector.kt`: `EventRules` (riseDb, riseWindowSeconds, endMarginDb,
  maxDurationSeconds, holdAfterMax; presets `V2`, `V3`); the detector takes `rules` (default V2).
  `EngineConfig.eventRules` (default V2) passes it. The rise is measured on contiguous valid
  audio (invalid audio empties its history).
- App: `EditionDetector.rules(context)` — public editions: always V2; Labor editions: V3, or V2
  when Einstellungen › Labor › «Lärmspitzen-Regeln 0.9.5» is off (Labor preference
  `event_rules_v3`, default on; read at the start of a measurement). `MeasurementService` passes it
  to the engine; the Labor manifest's `session_start` records `eventRules` (`v2`/`v3`) and the
  five values.
- The detector code is **shared** by all editions; the public editions' behaviour does not change
  because they keep the V2 default (`DetectorRulesEditionTest`). No privacy promise is affected
  (detector thresholds; PRIVACY.md unchanged).

## 6. Not verified

- Anything on a phone: CPU cost of the rise window (24 values per tick, negligible on paper), the
  switch in the Labor settings, the manifest lines of a real session.
- Two nights, two phones, two places; 48 labelled events (35 vehicle/loud). One miss more or less
  changes the percentage by 3 points. The thresholds are provisional like the ones before.
- The replay works on AAC-decoded 16 kHz audio; on the phone (48 kHz PCM) a few threshold-edge
  events differ (see 1).
- Whether the events V3 drops are really distant traffic: only 44 of the 415 swells are dropped,
  and none of them is labelled. Listening to a sample of them would settle it.
- The «loud vehicle» part of ros 632 being inside V3's 30 s (see 4).

## Reproduce

```
ffmpeg -i continuous/20261017_03.m4a -ac 1 -ar 16000 wav/20261017_03.wav     # every hour file
./gradlew :dsp:replayNight --args="--manifest manifest.jsonl --wavs wav --session 20261017_024754 \
    --out out/kal_base --excess 6.5 --floor 45.5 --offset manifest --head none --ticks out/kal.ticks"
./gradlew :dsp:replayNight --args="--manifest manifest.jsonl --wavs wav --session 20261022_113420 \
    --from 20261022_21 --to 20261023_06 --out out/ros_base --excess 6.5 --floor 30 --offset manifest \
    --head none --ticks out/ros.ticks"
./gradlew :dsp:replayRules --args="--ticks out/kal.ticks --variants variants.csv --floor 45.5 --out out/kal_rules.csv"
./gradlew :dsp:replayNight --args="… --rules v3"                                # full-engine check
```

`variants.csv`: `name,excess,rise,rise_window,end_margin,max_dur,hold`, e.g.
`v3,6.5,3.5,3,3,30,true` or `v2,6.5,0,3,nan,300,false`. The label matching and tables were made
with small Python scripts outside the repository (labels, audio and manifests are not in it).
